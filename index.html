Frequently asked questions

What is a mortgage payment made of?

The payment covers principal and interest. Taxes, insurance and any mortgage insurance are usually extra, and are often collected through escrow.

How much deposit do I need?

It varies by lender and country. A 20% deposit is a common target because it usually avoids extra insurance costs, but many loans allow smaller deposits.

Is a 15-year or 30-year mortgage better?

A 15-year loan costs more each month but far less interest overall. A 30-year loan costs less monthly. The right choice depends on what you can afford comfortably.

Can I pay my mortgage off early?

Usually yes, by making extra payments. Check your loan terms first, because a small number of older loans charge a prepayment fee.

Does this calculator include taxes and insurance?

No. The monthly payment shown is principal and interest only. Add your local taxes and insurance to get the full monthly cost.

Are the results a lending decision?

No. The results are planning estimates. A lender makes its own decision based on your income, debts, credit and the property.
